# HR Policies and Benefits

## Leave Policies

### Annual Leave (PTO)
- Unlimited PTO policy for all full-time employees
- Minimum recommended: 15 business days per year
- Maximum consecutive: 20 business days (manager approval required)
- Submit requests via Workday at least 2 weeks in advance
- Team calendar must be updated before departure

### Sick Leave
- Separate from PTO, no cap on sick days
- No documentation required for < 3 consecutive days
- Doctor's note required for 3+ consecutive sick days
- Extended illness: Short-term disability kicks in after 10 days

### Parental Leave
- **Primary caregiver**: 20 weeks paid leave
- **Secondary caregiver**: 10 weeks paid leave
- Flexible return-to-work options (gradual ramp-up)
- Benefits continue during leave
- Must be employed 6+ months to qualify

### Other Leave Types
- Bereavement: 5 days for immediate family, 3 days for extended family
- Jury duty: Full pay for up to 2 weeks
- Volunteer: 2 days/year paid volunteer time
- Military: Per USERRA regulations

## Compensation

### Pay Philosophy
- Target 75th percentile of market for base salary
- Salary bands reviewed annually against Radford survey data
- Pay equity analysis conducted bi-annually

### Bonus Structure
- Annual performance bonus: 10-20% of base salary
- Paid in Q1 for previous year performance
- Based on company performance + individual performance rating

### Equity
- RSU grants for all full-time employees
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
- Refresh grants annually for strong performers
- ESPP: 15% discount on company stock purchases

## Performance Management

### Review Cycles
- Annual performance review: January
- Mid-year check-in: July (informal)
- New hire 90-day review

### Rating Scale
| Rating | Description | Bonus Impact |
|--------|-------------|-------------|
| Exceptional | Top 10%, exemplary impact | 150% of target |
| Exceeds | Above expectations consistently | 120% of target |
| Meets | Consistently delivers | 100% of target |
| Developing | Below expectations, improvement plan | 0-80% of target |
| Unsatisfactory | Significant performance gaps | 0% of target |

### Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)
- Duration: 60-90 days
- Weekly check-ins with manager and HR
- Specific, measurable goals documented
- Successful completion: performance concerns removed from record

## Code of Conduct

### Core Principles
1. **Respect**: Treat everyone with dignity
2. **Integrity**: Be honest and transparent
3. **Inclusion**: Embrace diversity of thought and identity
4. **Excellence**: Hold yourself and others to high standards
5. **Collaboration**: Share knowledge, lift others up

### Zero Tolerance Policies
- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Retaliation against those who report concerns
- Falsification of time or expense records
- Conflicts of interest without disclosure

### Reporting Concerns
- Direct: manager or skip-level manager
- HR: hr@company.com
- Anonymous: ethics hotline at 1-800-XXX-XXXX (third-party managed)
- Legal/Compliance: legal@company.com

## Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility
- Fully remote: engineering, product, design, data roles
- Hybrid (2 days/week in office): sales, customer success
- In-office required: facilities, IT, operations

### Remote Work Requirements
- Reliable internet: minimum 50 Mbps download
- Dedicated workspace (not public locations during confidential calls)
- Available during core hours: 10am-3pm in your timezone
- Camera on for 1:1s and team meetings (company norm)

### Equipment
- $1,500 home office setup stipend (one-time)
- $500/year refresh budget
- Company laptop shipped before start date
- Monitor, keyboard, mouse provided on request

## Learning & Development

### Annual L&D Budget
- $2,000 per employee per year
- Covers: courses, books, conferences, certifications
- Submit reimbursements in Expensify

### Internal Programs
- Weekly Tech Talks (Fridays, recorded)
- Engineering blog (encouraged to write)
- 20% time for innovation projects (quarterly cycles)
- Mentorship program (match with senior leaders)

### Tuition Reimbursement
- Up to $5,250/year (IRS maximum for tax-free benefit)
- Must maintain B grade or better
- 2-year commitment to company post-graduation
